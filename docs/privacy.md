# File Trail Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 24 November 2026*

File Trail does not collect, store, share or sell any information about you.

## What File Trail does not do

- **No data collection.** File Trail has no analytics, no usage tracking, no advertising and no crash-reporting service of its own.
- **No account.** There is nothing to sign up for or sign in to.
- **No network connections.** File Trail makes no network connections of its own. It doesn't check for updates or fetch anything from the web, including pictures that a Markdown file links to on the internet.

## What stays on your Mac

To work the way you set it up, File Trail keeps a few things on your Mac, in its own files in your Library:

- your settings, shortcuts, favorites and saved views;
- the folders you have visited, so Go To can suggest them;
- the record of recent file operations that Undo and an interrupted paste rely on;
- if you choose it, the windows and tabs to restore at the next launch.

None of this leaves your Mac, and removing those files removes it.

## When you use other parts of macOS

When you share a file, open it in another app, connect to a server or use Quick Look, macOS and the app you chose handle it, under their own privacy policies. If you have turned on sharing crash reports with app developers in System Settings, Apple may pass File Trail's crash reports on to its developer; they describe the crash, not your files.

## Questions

Questions about this policy can be asked on [File Trail's issue tracker](https://github.com/mdemirhan/filetrailnative/issues). If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date.
