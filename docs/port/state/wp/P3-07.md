## Goal

Undo and Redo of file operations, ported exactly. Undo only renames exclusively or moves to the Trash; it never overwrites or deletes (file-operations.md §0, §4).
- **History** (`undoHistory.ts`):
  - in memory, capped at 1,000,000 item steps, oldest evicted first but never the newest; an oversized single operation is kept;
  - newest first; Undo moves an entry to Redo and back; leftovers go on top; an all-failed operation is forgotten;
  - a new operation clears Redo; operations that changed nothing are ignored; Can't Undo until the next undoable one;
  - a change counter and notifications.
- **Recording.** Fill P3-05's undo hook with the coordinator's recording for each operation (§4.2, `writeOperations.undoLog`; original `pasteUndoLog` at `:316`, `recordFinishedWrite` at `:496` and `:880-900`). Rename and Trash (P3-06) and Paste (P3-03) produce the logs; you only record them. The record lands before the next operation can start; the slot is freed even if keeping the history fails. Replace the "not available yet" Undo and Redo methods P3-05 left on `WindowWriteActions` with the real ones.
- **Plan** (`undoPlan.ts`): the reverse of each step kind, and the checks before each step: the very item or a safe-saved file, put-back of only the very item, ids or stamps, disks that are gone or reconnected, names freed or filled by earlier steps. Also `findQuestions` (all or nothing; name taken: Cancel / Keep Both; changed: Cancel / Move to Trash; up to 5 names).
- **Execution** (`undoExecution.ts`; coordinator `writeOperations.ts:2114-2225`):
  - prepare without the slot, within 10 s, returning a per-window ticket;
  - start re-checks inside the slot;
  - stop and resume; failures kept on the list; Redo after a partial Undo;
  - links, locks and ACLs restored, names (case, normalization, packages numbered before the extension);
  - a Trash-less copy is never deleted.
- **UI**:
  - Undo and Redo labels in the Edit menu through `ApplicationMenuSync`'s `UndoHistoryMenu`. P4-01 built the files-vs-text-field pairs; a focused text field still undoes its typing, never the files.
  - The undo question wording from `undoQuestion.ts`. The alert itself is P6-01's: use P3-05's `WriteDialogs` seam.
  - The "Can't Undo" rules.
- **Differential.** Add an undo step to `tools/parity/exec`: after a paste scenario, the reference runs the app's Undo, then Redo, on the paste's undo log, with the given question answers. It records the tree, the Trash, the questions and the menu labels. Generate the golden **`exec-undo`**: Undo, then Redo, for **every undoable operation that exists when you run**: copy, cut (move), Replace, Move To, rename, Move to Trash and New Folder. At least 120 cases per operation kind (4x the old 30, per the 2026-10-14 assurance raise), each with and without outside changes between the operation and the Undo (the item renamed, moved, edited, trashed, its name taken, its disk ejected), across the question answers. The Swift engine must match it.
- **Sequences.** Generate a second golden, **`exec-undo-sequences`**: at least 200 seeded random sequences of 3-12 steps mixing those operations, Undo, Redo and outside changes, including a new operation after an Undo (Redo must clear), Undo past a failed or partial step, and Redo after a partial Undo. Compare the tree, the Trash, the questions and the Undo/Redo labels after every step, not only at the end.
- **Stop and crash during Undo and Redo.** Stop-injection at every step of an Undo and of a Redo; crash-injection mid-Undo followed by P3-04's journal recovery. Nothing may be lost or left half-moved, compared against the reference's behavior for the same injection point.
- **Real disks.** Undo and Redo of a copy, a move and a trash on FAT32, exFAT, case-sensitive APFS and across two volumes (`FTTestSupport.DiskImage`), and with the target disk ejected before the Undo.
