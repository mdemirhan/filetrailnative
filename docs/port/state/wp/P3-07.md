## Goal

Undo and Redo of file operations, ported exactly. Undo only renames exclusively or moves to the Trash; it never overwrites or deletes (file-operations.md §0, §4).
- **History** (`undoHistory.ts`):
  - in memory, capped at 1,000,000 item steps, oldest evicted first but never the newest; an oversized single operation is kept;
  - newest first; Undo moves an entry to Redo and back; leftovers go on top; an all-failed operation is forgotten;
  - a new operation clears Redo; operations that changed nothing are ignored; Can't Undo until the next undoable one;
  - a change counter and notifications.
- **Recording.** Fill P3-05's undo hook with the coordinator's recording for each operation (§4.2, `writeOperations.undoLog`). The record lands before the next operation can start; the slot is freed even if keeping the history fails.
- **Plan** (`undoPlan.ts`): the reverse of each step kind, and the checks before each step: the very item or a safe-saved file, put-back of only the very item, ids or stamps, disks that are gone or reconnected, names freed or filled by earlier steps. Also `findQuestions` (all or nothing; name taken: Cancel / Keep Both; changed: Cancel / Move to Trash; up to 5 names).
- **Execution** (`undoExecution.ts`; coordinator `writeOperations.ts:2114-2225`):
  - prepare without the slot, within 10 s, returning a per-window ticket;
  - start re-checks inside the slot;
  - stop and resume; failures kept on the list; Redo after a partial Undo;
  - links, locks and ACLs restored, names (case, normalization, packages numbered before the extension);
  - a Trash-less copy is never deleted.
- **UI**:
  - Undo and Redo labels in the Edit menu through `ApplicationMenuSync`'s `UndoHistoryMenu`. P4-01 built the files-vs-text-field pairs; a focused text field still undoes its typing, never the files.
  - The undo question wording from `undoQuestion.ts`. The alert itself is P6-01's: use P3-05's `WriteDialogs` seam.
  - The "Can't Undo" rules.
- **Differential.** Add an undo step to `tools/parity/exec`: after a paste scenario, the reference runs the app's Undo, then Redo, on the paste's undo log, with the given question answers. It records the tree, the Trash, the questions and the menu labels. Generate the golden **`exec-undo`**, at least 30 cases drawn from `exec-copy` and `exec-cut`, plus outside changes between the paste and the Undo. The Swift engine must match it.
