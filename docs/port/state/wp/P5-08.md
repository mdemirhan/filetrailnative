## Goal

Where the UI meets the write coordinator: the visible clipboard, paste, and drag and drop, ported exactly (B-CLIP).
- **Copy and Cut** (B-CLIP §1): what goes on the app's clipboard (top-level items only, `selectTopLevelItems`), through `ExplorerWindows`' `SharedClipboard`. It is told to every window and a new window opens with it (B-VIEW-184). It is also written to `NSPasteboard` as file URLs for other apps, as the original does.
- **Clipboard marks** (B-CLIP §2, ui.md §2.6):
  - the 12 pt copy or scissors glyph after the name (white on a selected row);
  - cut items dimmed (label 0.55, icon 0.45);
  - the 700 ms accent flash;
  - the Icons badge that P5-04 draws, fed from here;
  - all of it only while `markClipboardItems` is on; in the list, icons, compact list and tree.
- **Clipboard button and popover** (ui.md §3.6, B-CLIP §3): the count on P5-01's toolbar button, the popover (260-420 wide, grouped under folder headings, 10 rows of 24, remove one, Clear Clipboard, the keys), Show/Clear Clipboard.
- **The clipboard follows its items** (B-CLIP §4): renamed, moved, trashed, or moved away by another app (`draggedAway`).
- **Paste** (B-CLIP §5, §6):
  - the destination (`resolvePasteDestinationPath`);
  - busy refusals;
  - the lock taken at once, with "Pasting…" / "Preparing…";
  - "Preparing to Paste…" with Cancel;
  - analysis errors and issues ("Couldn't Paste" texts);
  - same-path items left out, and gone items dropped and named;
  - **no conflict:** start at once with the safe policy;
  - **conflict:** hand the report to a `PasteReviewPresenting` seam that P6-02's review sheet fills. Until then the seam cancels and says the review isn't available, so nothing is written unreviewed;
  - Duplicate (⌘D) and Move To;
  - a cut cleared once at its end, and by the window that adopts it.
  - The dialogs go through P3-05's `WriteDialogs` seam.
- **Progress and results on screen** (B-CLIP §8, §9; B-VIEW-171):
  - connect `WindowWriteActions`' events to P5-09's progress card and toasts;
  - progress in every tab; the selection of what arrived only where the operation started (P3-05 decides; you show it).
- **Drag out** (native-and-system.md §9, B-CLIP §11): `NSDraggingSource` from the list, icons, compact list and tree; file URLs only; the drag images (icon plus label pill, max 240 pt, a stack formation, animating back on cancel); within the app Copy and Move; `ignoreModifierKeys` only over the app's windows; the end mapped to Delete, Move, Copy or Link, then `findDraggedAway`.
- **Drag in and between windows** (B-CLIP §12, B-TREE §13):
  - drop targets on rows, folders, the pane background, sidebar rows and the path bar (which takes no files);
  - move versus copy by disk id, with ⌥ to copy and ⌘ to move, and the cursors;
  - spring-loaded folders, the hover-expand delays, the highlight;
  - files from other apps read from the drag pasteboard, more than 100,000 paths refused;
  - no file promises, as in the original;
  - bring the window to the front, or bounce the Dock icon after 250 ms;
  - drags refused while busy (`describeDragRefusedWhileBusy`).
  - Use `draggingSequenceNumber` (D-004). Springing a tab open is not available (D-003).
- Register a `prefs` handler for `markClipboardItems` with `AutomationPrefs.register`, from your own code under `#if DEBUG`.
