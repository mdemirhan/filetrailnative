## Goal

The Icons and Compact List views, ported exactly, plugged into P5-03's content pane shell and P5-05's selection model.
- **Icons** (ui.md §2.1, B-VIEW §2):
  - `NSCollectionView` with a fixed layout from `iconGridLayout.ts`: columns `max(1, floor((width − 32) / cellMin))`, stretched, items centered; icon 64/48, tile 72/56, row pitch 116/94, the paddings in the table.
  - Labels: 13/16 (Compact 12/15), at most two lines, middle truncation through `IconLabel` (P1-02).
  - Only the tile and label take clicks.
  - Selection: the tile at ink 10%, and the label pill in accent, or gray when unfocused or the window is inactive.
  - Thumbnails from `FileThumbnailService`/`ThumbnailLoadQueue` (P2-04): at most 4 at a time, cancelled when a cell is reused, photos framed with a hairline and shadow.
  - The clipboard badge: an 18 pt circle at (−4, −4) with a copy or scissors glyph, drawn from a per-item clipboard state that P5-08 feeds.
  - Items move to their new places over 220 ms when the Info panel slides (ui.md §1.1).
- **Compact List** (ui.md §2.3, B-VIEW §3):
  - column-major, scrolling horizontally, with the vertical wheel scrolling sideways;
  - item width 292/252, gap 18, rows 28/24, padding `8 10 4`;
  - the highlight hugs the content; 18 pt icons;
  - `computeRowsPerColumn` from `virtualization.ts`.
- **Keyboard and selection.** Implement P5-05's layout-navigation protocol for both views: grid arrows; ←/→ by a column and ↑/↓ by an item in Compact; paging; reveal. Use P5-05's selection model, marquee and inline rename. Don't build a second model.
- **Seams:** the same `contextMenuProvider` and drag hooks as the List (P5-09, P5-08).
- **Budgets:** scrolling 10,000 items in Icons with no main-thread frame over 16 ms; no synchronous disk access while drawing (PLAN.md §3.2, §4).
- Register `prefs` handlers for `viewMode` (icons and list) and `compactIconView`/`compactListView` with `AutomationPrefs.register`, from your own code under `#if DEBUG`.
