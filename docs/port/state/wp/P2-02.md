## Goal

Folder sizes, ported exactly: the 4-thread `getattrlistbulk` walker (same device, mount points skipped, firmlinks walked, unreadable folders counted as empty, incomplete marking, cancellation, streaming of finished folders); the job queue rules (one per window, automatic jobs at utility QoS, cache answers first, at most 256 finished jobs); `FolderSizeCache`; probe-many; adjusting cached sizes after writes; and Home Trash counting.
