Each was downloaded as the 1280-px-wide thumbnail and saved again as JPEG with the longest side at 1280 px; nothing else was changed. ## Notes

- **Fuji at dawn.jpg** was taken at sunset (17:49), not at dawn. No CC0 or public-domain dawn photo of comparable quality was found.
- **Arrabida coast.jpg** shows the cliffs of Cabo Espichel, the western end of the Arrábida range, not the better-known beaches below the Serra da Arrábida near Setúbal; no CC0 or public-domain photo of those was found.
- **Coast road.jpg**, **Fjord morning.jpg**, **Harbor sunrise.jpg**, **Island noon.jpg**, **Alpine lake.jpg**, **Desert dusk.jpg**, **Night ridge.jpg**, **Old town evening.jpg** and **Valley mist.jpg** have generic names; the places are those in the table.
